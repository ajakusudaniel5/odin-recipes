Stew

This is the stew recipe page, Click on recipe to return to the recipe page

This image describes a delicious looking bowl of stew

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes
  • Water
  • Onions
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Cube
  • Tomato paste
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Grind tomatoes,peper and onions
  2. Put a saucepan on fire and pour oil into it
  3. Slice onions into it
  4. Add tomato paste and stir
  5. Add grinded tomatoes,peper and onions
  6. Leave to cook for about 15 minutes
  7. Add salt and cube to taste
  8. Serve with rice or ampesi
