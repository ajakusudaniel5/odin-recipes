Rice Recipe

Description

The image above describes a bowl of cooked rice ready to be served

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Put a saucepan of water on fire
  2. Add a few cups of washed rice to the water
  3. Add oil and salt and stir
  4. Leave to cook for about 25 minutes
  5. Serve with stew or soup
