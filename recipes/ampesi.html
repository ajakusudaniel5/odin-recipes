Ampesi

This is the ampesi recipe page,Click on recipe to return to the recipe page

This picure describes boiled yam in a bowl ready to be served with stew

Ingredients

  • Yam
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Slice yam to sizeable pieces and add to water in a saucepan
  2. Add salt and put on fire
  3. Leave to cook
  4. Serve with stew
